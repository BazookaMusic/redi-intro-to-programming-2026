# Exercise 4: Bunny hops 🐰 and rocket launch 🚀

🟢 Beginner · Exercise 4 of 10 · [All exercises](../README.md)

This exercise has two parts, so it has two files: `part_a.py` and `part_b.py`. The tests check both.

`range()` can take a third number, the **step**. The step says how much to count by.

- `range(0, 10, 3)` gives `0, 3, 6, 9`. It counts up by 3.
- A negative step counts down. `range(3, 0, -1)` gives `3, 2, 1`.

The `stop` number is still never included.

**Rule for both parts:** Use a for loop.

**Part A, bunny hops:** In `part_a.py`, print the even numbers from 2 to 10.

**Expected output:**

```text
2
4
6
8
10
```

**Part B, rocket launch:** In `part_b.py`, print a countdown from 5 to 1. Then print `Liftoff!`.

**Expected output:**

```text
5
4
3
2
1
Liftoff!
```
